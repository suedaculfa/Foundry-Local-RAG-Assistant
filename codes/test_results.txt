Question : What are the advantages of Embeddings and Vector Search?
Expected : The assistant should answer using the documents.
Answer   : There are several advantages of embeddings and vector search compared to traditional keyword search:

1. **Semantic Understanding**: The system understands the meaning of text instead of matching exact words, making it more accurate in understanding the intent behind the query or question being asked.

2. **Improved Retrieval Accuracy**: Relevant documents can be found even when different vocabulary is used, allowing users to get better matches based on the actual content of the documents they are searching.

3. **Natural Language Queries**: Users can ask questions naturally without needing specific keywords, enhancing the efficiency and effectiveness of natural language interactions.

4. **Scalable Architecture**: With the help of specialized vector databases, developers can store and manage large amounts of data effectively, making it easier to scale up and down depending on the needs of different environments and scales.

By leveraging embeddings and vector search, AI systems can gain deeper insights from vast amounts of textual data, leading to more precise and efficient solutions to complex problems.
